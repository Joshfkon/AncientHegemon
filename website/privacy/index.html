Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Ancient Hegemon does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It shows no advertising and contains no analytics or tracking.
  • It does not connect to the internet, except to Apple's App Store for in-app purchases (below).

In-app purchases

The game is free. Expansions (new empires) can be bought in the app. Purchases are made and handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's privacy policy. The app asks the App Store for prices, to make or restore a purchase, and which expansions your Apple ID owns. We never receive your name, Apple ID, payment details or purchase history; Apple sends us only anonymous sales totals.

Your saved games

Your saved games and settings are kept only on your device, and are removed when you delete the app.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your browser's standard request information.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if online play is added, this page will be updated before the app changes.
