Thanks for playing Ancient Hegemon.

Get in touch

Found a bug, stuck on something, or have an idea? Email JoshuaFKon@gmail.com. If it's a bug, please include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what happened.

Getting started

  • Turn on Guided on the title screen for a slower opening. Your council will explain taxes, armies, kingdoms and diplomacy as you go.
  • How to play on the title screen covers the rules.
  • To march, tap your army's token, then tap one of the glowing lands next to it. To look at a land instead, tap the land itself.

Common questions

Where are my saved games? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Does it need the internet? No. The whole game plays offline.

Can I play the same map again? Yes. Every random world has a number, and you can choose a world by its number on the title screen. The classic Mediterranean map is always there too.
