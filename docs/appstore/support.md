# Ancient Hegemon: Support

Thanks for playing Ancient Hegemon.

**Need help, found a bug, or have an idea?** Email JoshuaFKon@gmail.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened.

**Getting started:** turn on *Guided* on the title screen for a slower opening where your council explains taxes, armies, kingdoms and diplomacy as you go. *How to play* on the title screen covers the rules.

**Saved games** are kept on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
