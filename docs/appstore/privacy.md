# Ancient Hegemon: Privacy Policy

*Last updated 23 October 2026*

Ancient Hegemon does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

- The app has no accounts, sign-in, advertising, analytics or tracking.
- It does not connect to the internet.
- Your saved games and settings are kept only on your device, and are removed if you delete the app.
