Thank you for reviewing Ancient Hegemon. It is an original game, adapted from a board game I designed myself, and was not built from a template or by an app generation service. My answers:

1. Ancient Hegemon is a single-player, turn-based strategy game set in the Late Bronze Age (about 1200 BC). The player leads Greece, Egypt, Italia or Mesopotamia against computer rivals. The challenge is keeping an empire fed, paid, supplied and loyal, not just winning battles. Armies eat grain and starve when cut off, bronze needs copper and tin from trade or conquest, standing armies drain the treasury, and conquered land is restless. Maps are generated fresh each game. Every independent land is a kingdom with its own ruler and memory that can be traded with, won over, made a vassal (it may refuse or later rebel), or conquered. Rivals are met in their throne rooms to negotiate marriages, pacts, trades and tribute. Battles use a 5×5 battlefield with formations, and successions can split an empire. It offers a deep strategy game on iPhone in short turns, offline, with no account, ads or timers.

2. Adults and older teens who enjoy turn-based strategy, history and board games, and want one to play on their phone in short sessions. An optional guided opening with in-game advisors helps newcomers.

3. Most mobile strategy games make territory everything. Here supply, resources and upkeep decide a realm's strength, so a small, well-built realm with good trade can win. Kingdoms act on their own and remember how they were treated, diplomacy happens face to face, and the setting, the Bronze Age collapse, is rarely covered. No ads, no purchases in this version, no data collection.

4. I tested it myself on TestFlight across many builds, playing complete games after each change, and invited a few other testers. Changes from that testing include a guided opening after feedback that the start was too abrupt, a fixed crash on an advisor's "Show me" button, separating selecting an army from inspecting a land, generated maps so openings differ each game, vassals that may refuse, shirk levies or rebel, trade with kingdoms priced by distance, army upkeep with defensive levies so building rivals conquest, and disbanding fleets.

5. Standalone. My other apps are separate products with their own codebases. Orbitfall – Star Conquest is a fast real-time space game of a few minutes per match, unlike this long turn-based historical campaign. Space Explorer: Silly Planets is a children's exploration game. All Seasons Stand is a casual drink-stand game. Deterrence is a branching Cold War crisis simulation. Headroom is a caffeine tracker, HyperTrack a workout tracker, and Team Priorities and Family Priorities are task tools. JoshToDo is an old unreleased to-do app.
