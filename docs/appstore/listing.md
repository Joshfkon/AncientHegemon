## Description
The Bronze Age is ending. Greece, Egypt, Italia and Mesopotamia each want to become the hegemon of the known world: the one power the others answer to.

Ancient Hegemon is a turn-based strategy game adapted from a board game, built for iPhone. It's easy to start and deep to master: every land, army and decision is on one map, and every game tells its own story.

A NEW WORLD EVERY GAME
• Play on a freshly generated world each time, or on the classic Mediterranean board
• Hunt for the tin and copper that make bronze, and the horses, timber, iron and wine that feed an empire

RULE A REALM, NOT JUST AN ARMY
• Grow grain, collect taxes, raise walls, build roads, farms, mines, harbours and granaries
• Keep your people fed and your legitimacy high, or face unrest, rebellion and civil war
• Plan the succession: heirs, regents, usurpers and contested crowns
• Fill your throne room with trophies and splendour that win prestige

A LIVING WORLD
• Every land outside the empires belongs to a kingdom with its own ruler, temperament and memory
• Win kingdoms with gifts, cow them into vassalage, or conquer them outright
• Vassals pay tribute and send warriors, until your power falters and they rise against you

DIPLOMACY FACE TO FACE
• Stand before rival rulers in their throne rooms
• Offer gifts, royal marriages, pacts and alliances, trade any goods for any goods, call for embargoes or demand tribute
• Rivals remember how you treated them

WAR ON LAND AND SEA
• Draw up your battle line and choose your plan before every battle
• Besiege walled cities, starve out garrisons, or sally out against the besiegers
• Build fleets, brave storms and pirates, and land armies on distant shores

FOR NEW RULERS AND OLD HANDS
• An optional guided opening and a council of advisors teach you as you play
• Four difficulty levels, historical scenarios, and AI rivals with their own personalities
• No ads, no accounts, no internet needed

Become the hegemon.

## Review notes
No sign-in, purchases or network access. To see the game quickly: on the title screen pick any empire (Guided on gives a slower, explained start), tap your army's token on the map, then tap a glowing neighbouring land to march. The throne room (crown button, top right) and the rivals' courts (people button) show the diplomacy.

## App Privacy
Data Not Collected. The app has no networking, analytics, ads or accounts; games are saved only on the device.
