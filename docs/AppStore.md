## Version information (App Store)

**Promotional text** [170]:

> The Bronze Age is ending. Raise an empire from a single city, keep its armies fed and its trade
> flowing, and hold it together as the old world falls apart.

**Description** [4000]:

> Ancient Hegemon is a game of conquest where the hard part is not winning battles but feeding the
> armies that win them.
>
> It is 1200 BC. Choose Greece, Egypt, Italia or Mesopotamia and start with a single city on a
> hand-painted map of the Mediterranean and the Near East. Forty territories lie between you and
> hegemony, held by rival empires and native tribes.
>
> RISK, WITH LOGISTICS
> • Every army eats. The further it marches from your capital, the more grain it needs.
> • Cut-off armies starve. Supply lines run over land and open sea and can be severed.
> • Peace opens trade routes that share copper, tin, horses and timber, and sea straits can be
>   held to tax or block them.
> • Bronze takes copper and tin together. Lose the tin trade and your armies go to war badly armed.
>
> BATTLES YOU CAN WATCH
> • Fights play out on a 5×5 battlefield with infantry, archers and cavalry.
> • Choose a formation, send a general, and see the odds before you commit.
> • Terrain, rivers, walls and morale all count. Replay any battle afterwards.
>
> AN EMPIRE TO RUN
> • Set taxes: more gold to invest, or slower growth and restless subjects.
> • Build roads, walls, farms, mines and harbors with gold, stone and timber.
> • Rulers age and die. Weak heirs bring regencies, ambitious generals bring civil war.
>
> KNOWLEDGE FROM EXPERIENCE
> There is no tech tree to pick from. Your people learn from what they do: sail and you master the
> sea, fight on horseback and you breed better horses, trade and you learn from your partners.
> Ironworking, Navigation, Chariots and more are discovered, not chosen.
>
> THE COLLAPSE
> From the middle of the game the world gets steadily worse. Sea Peoples raid the coasts, droughts
> wither the fields, earthquakes topple walls and trade routes break. Bronze wears out without
> tin; iron does not. Some empires will endure. Most will not.
>
> WAYS TO WIN
> Dominate the map, become the richest trading power, or simply be the empire history remembers
> when the age ends. Victories come in decisive, major and minor grades.
>
> Single player against computer rivals. Plays in landscape. Your game saves automatically.

## TestFlight test information

> • Did the collapse (Sea Peoples, droughts, earthquakes, broken trade) feel fun or just punishing?
> • Did anything crash, freeze, or look wrong? Use TestFlight's screenshot feedback to show us.
